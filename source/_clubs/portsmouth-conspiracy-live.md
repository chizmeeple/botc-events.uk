  Cosham games have a new home at Made By Me Crafts on Fareham's West Street, within sight of the train station. Entry is £5; drinks and snacks are available on site.
  Step into the mysterious town of Ravenswood Bluff, where demons stalk the night and whispers fill the air.
  Blood on the Clocktower is a thrilling social deduction game for 5–20 players, so join us for a night of mystery, bluffing, and strategy.
  Take on a unique role as either Townsfolk, Outsider, Minion, or even the Demon itself.
  Your goal?
  • If you’re good: uncover the truth before it’s too late.
  • If you’re evil: spread lies, cause chaos, and survive at all costs.
  What makes Blood on the Clocktower different from other Social Deduction games?
  • Every player has a unique ability and an important part to play
  • Private conversations are allowed, and encouraged
  • Even in death, you still have influence and can participate in the game…
  Your friendly neighbourhood Storyteller will guide you through the rules, so no prior experience is needed.
